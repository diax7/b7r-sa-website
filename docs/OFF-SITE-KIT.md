# The off-site kit (ADR-050, project 4): a draft for Dhia's review

What the visibility score cannot see and only Dhia can do (ADR-049 R1, the five boxes of
Visibility → Off-site checklist): the profiles and posts outside b7r.sa that an answer engine
reads to corroborate what the site says. Every piece below is ready to paste; the facts are
BRD §1.1 as of 2026-09-12 (five products, base costs 45 to 95 SAR, Jeddah, five days, Salla,
Zid, Shopify, 30 SAR welcome credit). Post, then tick the box; the score moves at once.

The wording is a draft under BRD §0.5: read it as your own voice and change what is not.

## 5. A first third-party mention (box: a first third-party mention)

The engines corroborate a brand by what others say. Ten places worth one message each, in
order of likely yield; the ask is the same everywhere: a listing or a mention with the site's
address and the sentence «منصة طباعة عند الطلب سعودية تطبع في جدة وتوصّل خلال 5 أيام».

| Where | What to send | Why it counts |
|---|---|---|
| Salla partners directory (the app store's listing page) | The app listing with the description of §1 and three screenshots | A page on salla.sa naming B7R with a link: the strongest corroboration for a Salla merchant's prompt |
| Zid partners directory | The same listing | The same, for Zid |
| Misk Foundation, Launchpad cohort 9 page | Ask for the cohort's company list to link the site | A `.org.sa` page naming the company |
| Saudi Business Center listing | The company entry with the website field filled | A government page with the address |
| The Salla merchants' community (the official forum or its Telegram) | A short post: what B7R does, the prices, the five-day promise, a link | Merchants ask assistants the same questions they ask there |
| The Zid merchants' community | The same post | The same |
| A Saudi e-commerce podcast (two: pick the ones that host Salla and Zid merchants) | An episode pitch: "print on demand made in Saudi Arabia: the numbers" | A transcript naming the brand |
| Three Saudi e-commerce newsletters | A two-line item with the site's address | A dated mention on a third-party domain |
| Wikipedia-style directories of Saudi startups (the Misk and MCIT-adjacent lists) | An entry | A neutral page |
| A guest post on a merchant's blog (a customer who sells with B7R) | Their story, with a link | The most quotable kind: someone else's words |

Tick the box on the first one that goes live; the rest compound.

---

Once a piece is posted, add its address to Site settings → the profiles where it belongs
(LinkedIn, YouTube, X): I2 reads the profiles as links and the `OnlineStore` schema names them
as `sameAs`, which is how an engine ties the profile to the site.
