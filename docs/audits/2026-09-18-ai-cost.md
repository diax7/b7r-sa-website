# AI cost audit (2026-09-18)

Phase 0 of `docs/plans/2026-09-18-prelaunch-programme.md`: every scheduled job and its cost
driver, the prompts' periods, the models on each connection and their cheaper equivalents, the
engine's model, and a monthly estimate before and after. Read-only: the code on `main`
(8ed8490) and the review database, read once on 2026-09-18 02:34 UTC by a Local API script
that selected no key and wrote nothing; no job was triggered and "Run now" was not pressed.
The measured facts are the `ai-runs` rows of the two "Run now" batches of 2026-09-16; the
prices are the code's own (`MODEL_RATES`, `searchFeeFor`), which is what the estimate on a run
uses, never the vendors' invoices.

## Summary

- One scheduled job spends money: the citation ledger, every morning at 07:00 Riyadh, on
  the three AI connections. Everything else is free (the pulls, the tick while the engine is
  off, the digest, IndexNow, the traffic counter).
- Measured: one batch of the 22 prompts costs $0.45 on OpenAI `gpt-4.1-mini`, $0.57 on Google
  `gemini-3.1-pro-preview` and $1.62 on Anthropic `claude-sonnet-4-5`: **$2.64 a day, $80 a
  month** at the daily period every prompt carries today (`everyDays: 1` on all 22 rows and
  in the seed).
- Where the money goes: on OpenAI the web search fee (84% of the batch, $25 a thousand on the
  mini and nano models); on Anthropic the input tokens (64%: the pages one search returns are
  the prompt, 15,800 tokens an answer); on Google the output tokens (62%: Gemini 3.1 Pro
  thinks, and thoughts are billed as output at $12 a million).
- The Anthropic connection has **no monthly limit** and is the dearest engine ($49 a month
  alone at the daily period): nothing brakes it. Its last Test (2026-09-17 21:01 Riyadh)
  failed with a decryption symptom, so its key may be unreadable today; the two others were
  last tested before the 2026-09-17 deploys.
- The daily cadence has not started costing yet on the review server: a stale queued
  `citation-ledger` job (id 181, `waitUntil` Monday 2026-09-21 01:00 UTC, queued by a
  Riyadh-clock process under the old weekly cron) blocks the daily schedule until it fires.
  That is the window to change the rows.
- **Recommended for Phase 3: every prompt weekly (`everyDays: 7`, the seed too), Google on
  `gemini-3-flash-preview`, Anthropic on `claude-haiku-4-5`, OpenAI unchanged, a limit on
  every AI connection ($10 / $5 / $5). Expected: about $6.20 a month for the ledger, $3.20
  with Claude off, against $80 today; the engine adds nothing until Level 4 and about $0.50 to
  $0.90 a month at one post a day once it is on.**
