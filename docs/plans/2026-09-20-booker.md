# The booker: a Cal.com-grade booking experience, in our brand (2026-09-20)

Dhia, 2026-09-20, after booking a test slot: "it needs much better UI/UX in all the steps from
the client side; similar to the premium options like Cal.com itself or the same level; very
nice, smooth, easy, professional; this is so important." PR 4b (ADR-062) shipped the
mechanism (rules, slots, Google, e-mails, the manage link, the sweep) behind a functional but
flat picker: a strip of days, pills of times, a form appended under them, a plain success
card. This plan replaces the client-facing surfaces only; nothing in the routes, the store,
the calendar client, the e-mails or the sweep changes.

## The reference, in one paragraph

Cal.com's booker is one card in three panes. The **event pane** holds the host's photo and
name, the event's title, and meta rows with icons (duration, the video link, the timezone).
The **calendar pane** is a month grid with ‹ › navigation, available days marked, the
selected day filled, today ringed. The **times pane** lists the selected day's slots down a
column; a tap on a slot splits it into the time and a **Confirm** half. Confirm swaps the two
right panes for the **form** (name, e-mail, notes, a back arrow) while the event pane gains the
chosen time. The **success** view says "This meeting is scheduled", lists What / When / Who /
Where with icons, offers Add to calendar (Google, Outlook, Office 365, ICS) and "Need to make a
change? Reschedule or Cancel". On a phone the panes stack, and the times list appears under
the grid once a day is chosen. Every transition is a short cross-fade; loading is a skeleton,
never a spinner over nothing.

## The booker, ours

- **One component, three modes**: `page` (`/book`), `inline` (the contact card, the event
  pane collapsed to a header row), `reschedule` (the manage page, the current slot marked and
  the form skipped). One card on the surface tone, `--shadow-island`, the 13 px corner, the
  three panes divided by hairlines; ≥ 1024 px three columns (event 280 px · calendar 1fr ·
  times 220 px), 768–1023 two rows (event over calendar + times side by side), under 768 one
  column with the times list unfolding under the grid when a day is chosen and the page
  scrolling to it.
- **Event pane**: the host from a new `host` relationship on the `booking` global to the
  `authors` collection (Dhia's record already carries the bilingual name and role and the
  photo, and is the one place they are maintained; the seed points at it; read at depth 1;
  an initials disc in the accent tint when the photo is empty), the event `title`, then meta
  rows with monochrome icons: the duration («30 دقيقة»), «Google Meet», the timezone («توقيت
  الرياض (GMT+3)» / "Riyadh time (GMT+3)"), and a one-line `blurb` (a bilingual field on the
  global; seed: «نجاوب على أسئلتك ونساعدك تبدأ»). `hostEmail` stays the technical field it is.
  From step 2 on, a row with the calendar icon and the chosen time («الاثنين 21 سبتمبر،
  10:00 ص»), and a «رجوع» link that returns to the times.
- **Calendar pane**: a month grid, Sunday first, the weekday initials of the locale, the
  month name with ‹ › (mirrored in RTL) bounded by the horizon; today ringed; a day with free
  slots carries a small accent dot and is a real button; a closed, past or full day is muted
  and not focusable; the selected day is the filled accent disc. Keyboard: arrows move, Enter
  selects, `role="grid"` with `aria-selected`, an `aria-live` line announcing the month. The
  month's open days come from one new endpoint `GET /api/bookings/days?month=YYYY-MM` (rules,
  closed dates, notice, horizon and the day's bookings against `maxPerDay`; no Google call:
  Google decides the exact slots on the day click, so a day can open and then show the empty
  state when the host is busy all day); it answers each day's **free count**, not a boolean
  (the grid mutes a full day; the times pane's skeleton has the right number of rows); a month
  outside today's and the horizon's is refused; cached 60 s, rate limited like `slots`. RTL:
  ‹ › mirror by meaning (previous is always toward the past, the icon chosen by `dir`), the
  arrow keys move by reading direction (Left is "next" in RTL); the keyboard e2e runs in
  Arabic too.
- **Times pane**: the selected day as a header («الاثنين 21 سبتمبر»), the slots down a
  column as full-width buttons («10:00 ص»), hover lifts, a skeleton of six rows while the day
  loads, an empty state («لا مواعيد متاحة في هذا اليوم، اختر يوماً آخر»), the busy-day case
  from Google the same. A tap splits the button: the time on one half, «تأكيد» on the other
  (Cal.com's pattern), under three accessibility conditions: the split is two real buttons,
  the time with `aria-pressed` and the confirm focusable the moment it appears (Enter on a
  time reveals it, Tab reaches it); a tap elsewhere collapses it as a pointer rule only,
  never on focus-out; the reveal is a width transition under `prefers-reduced-motion`, not a
  re-mount, so focus stays. «تأكيد» goes to the form.
- **Form step**: the calendar and times panes cross-fade into the form (200 ms, 8 px, honoured
  by `prefers-reduced-motion`): name, phone, e-mail, «ملاحظات إضافية» (optional), the §4.11
  labels, placeholders and validation under the fields with `aria-invalid`, the Turnstile
  invisible, the button «تأكيد الحجز» with its pending state («جارٍ الحجز»); the refusals in
  place (slot taken → back to the times with the day refreshed and a line saying so; off-grid,
  rate limit, challenge as today).
- **Success view**: a check disc that scales in once, «موعدك محجوز», «أرسلنا التفاصيل إلى
  بريدك», then the rows with icons: **ماذا** (the title), **متى** (the day, the time range,
  Riyadh), **مع من** (the host and the merchant), **أين** («Google Meet» with the link as a
  button when present, «الرابط يصلك على بريدك قبل الموعد» when it follows), the note if any;
  **أضف إلى تقويمك**: three links, Google Calendar (the `calendar.google.com/calendar/r/eventedit`
  link), Outlook (one deep link serves outlook.live.com and outlook.office.com) and the `.ics`
  (the existing route), each an icon button; **تحتاج تغييراً؟** «غيّر الموعد» «ألغِ الحجز»
  (the manage link). The "who" row shows the merchant's name from the form, never from a
  later read.
- **Manage page**: the same card; the event pane with the booking's summary and the status
  pill; «غيّر الموعد» opens the calendar and times panes inline in `reschedule` mode (the
  current slot marked and not selectable, the same notice rule on both ends, the confirm
  reads «تأكيد التغيير» and patches); «ألغِ الحجز» opens the
  site's `Dialog` («إلغاء الحجز؟» with the consequence, «ألغِ الحجز» / «رجوع»); the cancelled
  and past states as full-card messages with a link to book again.
- **Contact card**: the inline mode, the booker's header row in place of the old card's title,
  the same steps inside the card, the WhatsApp fallback unchanged while the switch is off.
- **Motion and polish**: the pane transitions, the split-button reveal, the slot rows
  staggered in (60 ms, capped at 8, like the reveal), focus rings on everything, hover and
  pressed states from the button primitive, no layout shift between steps (the card keeps its
  height across the form step on desktop by a min-height matched to the calendar pane).
- **Loading**: on `/book` the card is the hero, so the island mounts eagerly (the loader's
  `eager` mode) over a stand-in that is the **real event pane rendered on the server** (photo,
  name, role, title, meta rows, blurb: the LCP text and image) with skeletons of the same
  dimensions for the calendar and times panes; the island hydrates over it with no shift. The
  contact card keeps the near-viewport loading.
- **Weight**: the island's own chunk gets a line in the budget spec: measure the current
  picker's chunk first, set the line at that plus the new panes (about 35 KB gzipped for the
  grid, the form, the success view and the URL builders), and the e2e reads the chunk's bytes,
  not only the first paint's.
- **Words**: every new string in both copy banks under ux-araby, BRD §4.19 rewritten (still
  `TODO(copy)` for Dhia's read), Western digits, Riyadh time with ص/م.
- **Not in scope**: a timezone picker (every merchant is on Riyadh time; the line says so), a
  12/24 h toggle, guests, a week or column layout, a custom success URL, an Office 365 link of
  its own, host fields of our own (the author record is the host), screenshots in the repo
  (a scratch folder linked from the PR body).
