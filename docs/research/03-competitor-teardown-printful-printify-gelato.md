## Patterns worth stealing (adapted for an Arabic-first Saudi POD site)

1. **Two-price product cards ("From X / From Y with Premium")** (Printify) → makes the upgrade tangible → Adapt only if B7R ever has tiers; otherwise show one honest base price.
2. **Inline "See how much you can make" calculator on homepage and pricing page** (Printify: product chips → "Sell it for" → "Daily sales" → monthly earnings) → converts "no upfront cost" into a number in 3 seconds → RTL version with product chips (تيشيرت، هودي…), inputs "سعر البيع" and "مبيعات يومية", output "أرباحك الشهرية التقديرية"; realistic Saudi retail defaults; footnote on VAT/shipping.
3. **Margin-first calculator variant** (cost + extra costs + desired margin % → retail price + profit) → matches how beginners think → Offer a toggle: "أدخل سعر البيع" vs "أدخل هامش الربح".
4. **Worked profit examples in copy** ("cost $8.80 → sell $14.67 → profit $5.87" at 40/50/60%) → concrete arithmetic beats adjectives → Three named products with real SAR numbers on category pages.
5. **Explicit profit equation as a design element** ("Printful price + Your profit margin = Retail price"; "Your profit = Retail price – Printful cost" as 4 numbered lines) → removes the #1 confusion (who pays whom, when) → Arabic three-tile equation "سعر بَحر + هامش ربحك = سعر البيع" with a 4-line money flow.
6. **Named, numbered how-it-works with one verb per step and a CTA per step** (Printify "Select → Create → Publish → Make money risk-free") → "اختر → صمّم → انشر → اربح" with per-step buttons; mirror connectors in RTL.
7. **Proof chips under the H1 + "no credit card" microcopy + third-party rating logo** (Printify "100% Free to use · 1300+ products · Global delivery", "4.8 on Shopify") → "مجاني 100% · بدون حد أدنى · توصيل لكل مدن المملكة" plus a real rating source (Salla App Store rating, Google reviews), never unattributed stars.
8. **Local-production stat trio** (Gelato "90% produced locally / 90% arrive within 5 days / 250+ partners") → speed and locality are the buying reason → "يُنتج في جدة · يصل خلال X أيام · تغطية كل مناطق المملكة"; carrier logos in the footer like Printful's USPS/DHL/FedEx.
9. **Live-order ticker and "X% recommend" on PDPs** (Printful) → social proof at the decision point → "آخر طلب من الدمام قبل 3 دقائق" (city-level only) once volume exists.
10. **Card badge vocabulary: Bestseller / New / Early Access / Eco-friendly** → fixed Arabic set: "الأكثر مبيعاً", "جديد", "وصول مبكر"; badge sits top-right in RTL.
11. **Integrations as grouped cards with one benefit line each, plus region tags** → Groups "منصات المتاجر" (Salla, Zid, Shopify) with tags like "متاح الآن" / "قريباً" and a one-line benefit ("سلة: مزامنة الطلبات تلقائياً خلال دقائق").
12. **Store-switching migration offer** (Gelato Velocity Switch, Printful "Switch to Printful", Printify "Transferring") → "انتقل إلى بحر" page for merchants currently on Printful/Printify.
13. **Resource Center with tabs (Blog | Guides | Webinars) plus topic hubs and an Academy** → `/blog/<category>/` clean paths, hubs, "أكاديمية بحر" of short Arabic videos; each post opens with "أهم النقاط" (Gelato's "Main takeaways").
14. **Footer as a second sitemap with payment + carrier badges** (Printful) → 5 columns, mada/Visa/Apple Pay/Tabby/Tamara icons, carrier logos, VAT + CR line, SBC badge, app-store badges.
15. **Audience switcher in the hero** (Gelato "For creators / For print producers") → Tabs "للمبدعين والمتاجر" / "للشركات والجهات" (bulk merch, corporate gifting), each with its own CTA.

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- **Nav that hides the catalog behind app login** (Printify's "Catalog" → app). Serve an SSR catalog with real prices; it is the biggest SEO surface.
- **Inconsistent numbers across pages** (Printful 143M/143.4M/143.9M; Gelato 250+/140+/130+; Printify 1300+ vs 2,000+). Keep one source of truth injected into every template.
- **Homepage with no proof or path** (Gelato: no testimonials, calculator, FAQ or integration wall; two promo banners and an upsell before any product).
- **Homepage body that vanishes without JS** (Printful renders hero + steps, then footer). Progressive enhancement matters for Saudi mobile traffic on mid-range Android.
- **Copy-paste modular blocks across 50 pages** (Gelato), reads as filler.
- **Long English SEO essays disguised as product pages** (Gelato /products/*). Keep marketing pages short; put depth in the blog/guides.
- **Price display without location context** (Printful "$11.92" until you pick a destination). Show SAR, shipping "+ شحن من 20 ر.س", delivery ETA up front; VAT-inclusive by default per ZATCA norms.
- **Promo bar + geo-switch + cookie + chat all stacked** (Gelato). One bar maximum; never auto-prompt a region switch to Arabic users already on the Arabic site.
- **Upsell inside the calculator result** (Printful's "with Printful Growth" second figure) can feel like bait.
- **Untranslated third-party widgets and mixed numerals**, decide once on Western digits and Arabic text, mirror all icons/arrows, localize the rating widget, chat bubble (bottom-left in RTL) and app badges.
- **"As seen in" with logos only** (Printify), without links or dates it reads as decoration.
