# Saudi / GCC Print-on-Demand Market: Website & SEO Positioning Research

> Research report generated 2026-09-12 by a background agent (Arabic + English searches). Input for the B7R marketing-site BRD. Not the BRD itself. Keyword ranking is ordinal (no public KSA volume dataset for the POD phrases surfaced); verified volumes exist only for the gift cluster.

## 0. Headline findings

1. **The KSA POD field is thin and immature.** Only one true POD app is discoverable in the Salla App Store ("أطبع لي" by مطابع نقاء, 39 SAR/mo, zero reviews). B7R has no discoverable listing in either the Salla or Zid app markets (search-based). Most "competitors" are custom-print shops (WhatsApp-quote, MOQ) borrowing POD language.
2. **International POD is structurally weak in KSA.** Printful puts Saudi in its "Worldwide" zone ($12.49 first item + $6 each additional, 10-20 business days transit on top of 2-5 days production, DDU customs). Printify has no MEA print providers (10-30 days). Neither integrates natively with Salla or Zid.
3. **Salla + Zid are the merchant acquisition channel.** Salla reports 68k+ active merchants and $13.3B GMV; Zid ~13.5k merchants and 10B+ SAR sales. A Salla/Zid app listing is a discovery channel, not just an integration.
4. **Arabic vocabulary is split.** Saudi providers and Salla itself say "طباعة حسب الطلب"; educational content and B7R say "الطباعة عند الطلب"; "برنت اون ديماند" is effectively unused (Google returns Brent crude). Target both Arabic forms; transactional money terms are "طباعة تيشيرت", "تيشيرت اليوم الوطني", "هدايا مخصصة".
5. **Trust signals are legally defined:** CR number, VAT certificate, return policy honouring the 7-day right, contact channel, privacy policy, and Saudi Business Center e-store authentication (replaced Maroof in 2023).

## 3. Platforms & ecosystem

### 3a. Storefront platforms

| Platform | What | Scale (published) | Notes for B7R |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Salla** | Hosted SaaS, Makkah, 2016; $130M pre-IPO (2024) | 68k+ active merchants, $13.3B processed, 15M consumers, 900+ apps (official); Arab News: 80k (Dec 2024) | Plans: Basic free / Plus 99 / Pro 299 SAR/mo. | **Zid** | Hosted SaaS, Riyadh, 2017; $59M raised | 12,000+ active merchants (+30% YoY); 13,500 merchants, 10B+ SAR sales; app market 446 apps. A "150,000 merchants" figure is unverified | Plans from ~100 SAR/mo; Zid Ship, ZidPOS. **Second channel.** |
| **Shopify** | Global | ~4.2k KSA stores | Cross-border/brand-driven merchants. Third channel. |

Market-share trackers disagree wildly because Salla stores on custom domains are under-detected. Defensible: Salla and Zid dominate Saudi SME retail; Shopify serves export-minded brands.

### 3b. Payments merchants expect
- E-payments = 85% of retail transactions in 2025 (SAMA); mada e-commerce SAR 69.3B in Q1 2025 (+56% YoY).
- Checkout preference (Worldpay 2025): Apple Pay 36%, mada 22%, STC Pay 12%; cards ~18%. Apple Pay is near-mandatory.
- STC Pay became stc Bank (Jan 2025). Google Pay launched on mada in 2025.
- BNPL: Tabby + Tamara hold ~93% of KSA BNPL; lift AOV 20-40% in fashion.
- Gateways: HyperPay, PayTabs, Moyasar, Tap; all native in Salla/Zid.
- Implication: show mada, Apple Pay, STC Pay, Tabby/Tamara logos on the merchant-facing page.

### 3c. Shipping carriers and aggregators
- Carriers: SMSA (fastest in big cities), Aramex (widest coverage), Naqel, J&T, iMile, SPL/سبل (Saudi Post), Zajel, DHL/FedEx, RedBox lockers.
- Typical merchant cost 14-35 SAR per parcel; COD fee 4-8 SAR.
- Aggregators: **OTO** (tryoto.com; 450+ carriers, B7R's actual provider), **Torod**, Salla Shipping and Zid Ship, Barq.
- **"Tryvo" is not a courier**: tryvo.net is a Riyadh school-transport app. The brief meant OTO.

### 3d. Trust signals Saudi visitors expect (legal baseline)
Per the E-Commerce Law (Royal Decree M/126, 2019), ZATCA and Ministry of Commerce:
- Store name, address/contact, and **commercial register number** displayed; e-store added to the CR within 30 days.
- Total price **including VAT and shipping**; **VAT certificate** displayed if registered (mandatory above 375k SAR revenue).
- **سياسة الاستبدال والاسترجاع** consistent with the consumer's 7-day withdrawal right; "لا يوجد إرجاع" wording is itself a violation.
- Privacy policy aligned with PDPL (SDAIA).
- **E-store authentication moved from Maroof to the Saudi Business Center (business.sa) in March 2023**; consumers verify via my.gov.sa. Use "توثيق المركز السعودي للأعمال"; mention Maroof only as the legacy name.
- Penalties up to 1M SAR and site blocking.
- Observed best practice: Lana Designs and Pteam put SBC authentication number + VAT number in the footer.

## 4. Audience

**Who starts POD brands in KSA**
- Young founders: 38% of new e-commerce registrations are youth-owned (18-40), 47% women-owned (Monsha'at Q2 2025); ~70% of the population under 35. Riyadh 35-39% of registrations, then Makkah, Eastern Province.
- Creators/influencers: 80% of Saudis follow influencers on Snapchat; micro-creators run Snapchat-linked print stores.
- Existing Salla/Zid merchants adding merch (cafes, perfumers, gyms) and corporate/uniform buyers.
- Students: graduation, university clubs, back-to-school.
- Designers/artists currently pushed toward Redbubble/TeePublic by Arabic guides.

**Motivations**: side income "من البيت", "بدون رأس مال", testing a brand idea, seasonal cash (National Day, Eid), Vision 2030/Monsha'at framing.

**Objections (evidence-backed)**
1. Quality: "قماش التيشيرت ثقيل، الطباعة بس مقاس A4" (itba3ly review). Counter with fabric GSM, print area in cm, wash tests, sample orders.
2. Delivery time and cost: Printful 10-20 bd + $12.49 shipping + customs; a Saudi merchant's National Day batch arrived after the holiday. Counter with production location, days, carrier logos.
3. Minimums/deposits: quote shops require MOQ and 70% deposits. Counter: "بدون حد أدنى".
4. Upfront cost: DIY cutter-plotter setup is 13-17k SAR. Counter: 0 SAR to start.
5. Margin doubt: guides warn gross margins are 20-50%. Counter with a transparent price list and a profit calculator.
6. Trust: who prints, where, is it legal, will my customer see B7R's name? Counter with white-label proof, CR/VAT, SBC authentication, real photos.

**Messaging that resonates**: warm Saudi-flavoured Arabic (B7R already uses "خلي كل شيء يشتغل تلقائياً", "يجي", "يوصَل"), direct and numeric, "شريكك المحلي", entrepreneur-to-entrepreneur.

## Positioning & messaging recommendations

1. **Own "الطباعة عند الطلب في السعودية" and "طباعة حسب الطلب لمتاجر سلة وزد" as two separate H1 clusters.** Home uses the first; integration pages use the second.
2. **Lead with the local-speed proof Printful cannot make:** "نطبع في [المدينة] ونسلّم داخل المملكة خلال X أيام، بدون جمارك". Publish a comparison page vs Printful/Printify.
3. **Publish prices.** Nobody in KSA POD shows a clean per-product base price + suggested retail + margin. A public price table and Arabic profit calculator become link magnets.
4. **Ship a Salla app and a Zid app, and make the listings primary landing pages.** "أطبع لي" has zero reviews; B7R can win the category.
5. **Trust block on every page:** CR number, VAT number, SBC e-store authentication number, return/reprint policy, WhatsApp support hours.
6. **Show the product truth:** fabric GSM, print area in cm (not "A4"), DTF/DTG method, wash-test video, sample-order button.
7. **White-label as a headline benefit:** "يوصل الطلب باسم متجرك، لا باسمنا" with a photo of branded packaging.
8. **Build a seasonal calendar hub** (National Day, Founding Day, Flag Day, Ramadan/Eid, back-to-school, graduation, Riyadh Season, White Friday).
9. **Three personas, three entry paths:** "عندي متجر سلة/زد" (integration), "عندي جمهور" (creators/merch), "أبدأ من الصفر" (guide funnel).
10. **Saudi-dialect Arabic consistently; English secondary but present** ("Print on demand Saudi Arabia", "custom t-shirt printing Riyadh") as a single English landing at minimum.
