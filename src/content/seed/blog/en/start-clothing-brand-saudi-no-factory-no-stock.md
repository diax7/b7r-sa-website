You have the brand idea, the design is almost ready, and the only thing in the way is the belief that starting takes a factory or a pile of stock in your living room. The truth is simpler: with print on demand you sell the piece before it is printed, and you pay only for what actually sold.

## What do you need before the first product?

Three things: a name for your brand, one design at a quality fit for printing, and an online store on Salla, Zid or Shopify. No equipment, no stock, no starting capital. A B7R Print account is free, and you receive SAR 30 of welcome credit at sign-up to try your first product with. If you have no design yet, start with a simple text or logo.

## How does it go from order to shipment?

You place your design on the product inside the app, set your selling price and publish the product in your store ([the three steps in detail](/en/how-it-works)). When your customer buys, the order reaches us automatically; we print it in Jeddah, pack it and ship it under your store's name within 5 days at most. Your customer never sees the B7R Print name anywhere.

## Which product should you start with?

Start with [the essential T-shirt](/en/products/tee-essential): it costs SAR 45, the suggested selling price is SAR 89, an estimated profit of SAR 44 per piece before shipping and tax. Once the design proves itself, add the hoodie or the tote bag with the same design at no extra cost.

## One last tip

Do not wait for the perfect design. Launch one product this week, watch what your customers say, then adjust. The brands that succeed are the ones that start from a single piece.
