Print on demand is a simple business model: products appear in your store with your design, and no piece is printed until a customer buys it. No stock, no minimum order, no leftover pieces at the end of the season.

## How is it different from traditional printing?

With traditional printing you pay up front for a whole batch, say 100 T-shirts, then try to sell them. With print on demand you pay the cost of a single piece only when your design sells, and you keep the difference between the selling price and the base cost. The second difference is time: you publish the product today and start selling the same day, with no shipment or production to wait for.

## An example from a Saudi store

Say the owner of a Salla store in Riyadh designs a T-shirt in Arabic calligraphy. She publishes it in her store at SAR 89. When a customer in Dammam buys it, the order reaches B7R Print automatically; we deduct the base cost of SAR 45 from her wallet, print the piece in Jeddah and ship it to Dammam under her store's name within 5 days at most. Her estimated profit is SAR 44 before shipping and tax, and she never touched the piece.

## Who is this model for?

The designer who wants to test ideas, the creator who wants merch under their own name, and the store owner who wants to add custom products without risk. The rule is the same for all: start small, measure the results, then expand what works. The questions we hear most often are [collected on the FAQ page](/en/faq).
