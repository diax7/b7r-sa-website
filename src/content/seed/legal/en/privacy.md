At B7R Print we are committed to protecting your privacy and keeping your personal data secure. This policy explains how we collect, use, keep and share your data when you use the platform and its services.

## 1. Definitions and scope
- Personal data: any information that can identify you as an individual (such as your name, email, mobile number and address).
- The platform or the service: the website, the dashboard, the applications and any print-on-demand service we provide.
- This policy applies to how we handle the data of the platform's users inside and outside the Kingdom, under the Personal Data Protection Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

## 2. The data we collect
- Data you provide yourself: name, email, mobile number, store name, designs, and other data you enter in your account or in contact forms.
- Automatic data: IP address, browser type, operating system, the pages you visit, the date and time of access, and usage logs.
- Cookies and measurement tools: we use analytics tools to measure how the site performs; analytics cookies are switched on only with your consent from the consent bar.

## 3. How we use the data
- To provide the service and fulfil orders (printing, packaging and shipping).
- To contact you about orders, customer support and updates.
- To improve the platform, analyse usage and develop products.
- To send marketing offers with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

## 4. Sharing data with third parties
- We may share your data with service providers such as carriers, payment or email providers, to the extent needed to deliver the service.
- We never sell your data to outside parties.

## 5. Data retention
- We keep your data for as long as needed for the purposes it was collected for, or as the regulations require.
- When data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it securely.

## 6. Data security
- We take technical and administrative measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.
- We disclose your personal data only to those entitled by law, or when the competent authorities request it.
- In the event of a security breach, we notify you as the law requires.

## 7. Your rights
- To access your data and know what we hold.
- To ask for data to be corrected or completed when it is inaccurate.
- To ask for your data to be deleted when its purpose has ended or when you withdraw consent.
- To contact us about your data: contact@b7r.sa

## 8. Changes to this policy
- We may amend this policy at any time; updates are announced on the website, and material changes are communicated to you directly or through a prominent notice on the platform.
- Continued use of the platform after a change means acceptance of the new policy.
